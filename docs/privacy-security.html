Privacy & security

Your money stays yours

No servers, no tracking, no SMS permission. Here is exactly what is stored, where, and how it is protected.

What Rivero never does

  • No account, no sign-up, no server of ours. Rivero has no backend.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No bank login, no credentials, no screen scraping.
  • No READ_SMS or other SMS permission. Ever.
  • No upload of your data. The only network use is the optional Google Drive backup you start yourself.

Permissions

Camera / photos (when you choose)Bill photos, account pictures, bill scanning (text recognised on the phone).

How data is protected

At rest

The database is encrypted with AES-256 (SQLite3 Multiple Ciphers). Its key is generated on the phone and held by the platform keystore through Flutter Secure Storage. There is no plaintext fallback: if the database cannot open, Rivero shows an error instead of creating an unencrypted one.

In the capture queue

Notifications waiting for the app are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key that never leaves the Android Keystore.

In backups

Encrypted on the phone with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 210,000 rounds) using AES-256-GCM, with authentication. Wrong passphrase or tampering is detected and refused. Drive only ever holds an opaque file in the app's private folder.

On screen

Optional biometric lock, hide-amounts mode, and, while the lock is on, screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked.

What is stored, and for how long

DataKept
Accounts, transactions, plans, autopay, owed money, settingsUntil you delete them or use Delete all data
Raw bank message text (audit log)90 days, then purged. One-time passwords are never stored. Not included in backups.
Bill photos and account picturesUntil you remove them. Included in encrypted backups.
Capture queueUntil the app next opens (then processed and emptied). Cleared by Delete all data.

System-level protections

  • android:allowBackup="false": Android's own backup never copies the database without its key.
  • Release builds are signed with a release key and built in CI from the public source.
  • Quality gate on every change: formatting, strict static analysis (very_good_analysis) and the full test suite.

Threat model

RiskMitigation
Lost or stolen phoneEncrypted database, optional biometric lock, encrypted backups for recovery
Someone looking over your shoulderHide amounts; screenshot and preview blocking with lock on
Cloud account compromiseBackups are encrypted with a passphrase Google never sees
Malicious or promotional SMSIntent classifier and promotion filter; uncertain captures wait for your confirmation instead of changing balances
App data copied off the deviceBackup disabled at the OS level; key bound to the keystore

Your passphrase cannot be recovered. Rivero does not know it and has no server to reset it. Store it somewhere safe.

Responsible disclosure

Found a security issue? Please report it privately through the repository's Security tab rather than a public issue. The full policy, scope and what to expect are in SECURITY.md.
