Money model

How Rivero thinks about money

The rules and formulas behind every number, with a worked example, so you can trust (and check) what you see.

The mental model

Two tiers of money

Physical accounts are real: banks, cards, wallets, cash. Plan categories are intentions: how a salary should be split. They never create or duplicate money; the plan only moves real balances between accounts you track.

The bank is the truth

Spending money is what your spending account really holds. The plan is the "of": what you meant to have this cycle. A bank's "available balance" message always wins over arithmetic.

Safe to spend

Everything on Home comes from one calculation, shared by the card, the Plan tab, the breakdown sheet and the "Log this anyway?" check, so they can never disagree.

left to spend = spending account balance − plan money still to be paid out of it
safe until payday = left to spend − autopay due − card bills (+ money owed, if counted)
per day = safe until payday ÷ days to payday   (never below 1 day, never below ₹0)

The pieces

PieceWhere it comes from
Spending account balanceThe account linked to your spending category (or the salary account). Updated by captures, logged payments, balance updates and the bank's "Avl bal".
Plan money still to be paidUnpaid remainder of plan-only categories (rent, family) when they are paid out of the same account that you spend from. If your spending account is separate from the salary account, nothing is held back from it.
Autopay dueActive autopays dated from 5 days ago (overdue, not yet seen) to the end of the cycle. Weekly ones count once per remaining week. Variable ones reserve their cap. Autopays charged to a different bank account do not touch your spending account.
Card billsOutstanding balance on credit cards, if "keep money aside for card bills" is on, minus what is already set aside: a card can be paid from its own account (for example a Slice savings account you move each card spend into). What that account holds covers the bill first; only the rest comes out of spending money. Cards paid from the same account share its balance.
Money owedWhat friends owe you, added only if you turned on "Count money owed to me".
Money you oweBorrowed money you promised to pay back before payday (it has a date) is subtracted: that cash is already spoken for. Undated debts are shown but not reserved.
Days to paydayDays until the next pay day, minimum 1. Refreshes at midnight and when the app opens. A salary that arrived early starts the next cycle and counts to the pay day after it.

If no spending account is linked

Rivero falls back to the budget: planned + carried over − spent. Linking a spending account makes the real balance the truth instead.

Freshness

Verified the newest entry is under 24 hours old · Estimated 24 to 72 hours · Stale older than 72 hours. A negative result is shown as a deficit with ₹0 per day.

The salary plan

  1. Salary lands in the salary accountA captured credit (or you planning ahead) triggers the split.
  2. You choose the splitPercentages or amounts. The total can never exceed the salary: unallocated = salary − Σ categories.
  3. Apply moves tracked moneySalary → spending account, salary → a linked investment or savings account. Entries get deterministic ids per cycle, so re-applying adjusts them instead of duplicating.
  4. Plan-only categories waitRent and family change a balance when their real payment is recorded; a bank debit with the exact planned amount from the salary account is matched to its category (and asks you to confirm).

Safety rules

  • A plan never moves an account below zero.
  • Re-applying inside the same cycle is a revision: spending so far and carry-over stay as they were.
  • Carry-over is unspent spending money from the last cycle; overspending is not carried as debt.
  • "Already moved" is remembered per cycle, so applying again never moves the money twice.

What counts as spending

Counts as spendingNever counts
Expenses, cash spending, card purchases, rent/EMI/family paid through the plan (in totals), refunds (reduce spending)Transfers between your accounts, card-bill payments, ATM withdrawals, loans you gave, repayments to you, balance updates, borrowing and repaying, ignored or unconfirmed entries, shares of a split bill that others owe

Home's "Spent this cycle" leaves out rent and family paid through the plan, matching Safe to spend. Activity's Spends filter, Analytics and the totals all use the same rule.

Cards, owed money and transfers

  • Card purchase: raises the card's outstanding; reserved as a bill; does not touch the spending budget until paid.
  • Card bill from the bank: the bank debit and the card credit are linked as one transfer (in either arrival order); the card's outstanding falls once.
  • Split bill: others' shares stop being your spending at once; the money is credited back to the budget only when it arrives.
  • Credit card billing cycle: each card has a statement day and a due day. Rivero shows days left in the cycle, the next due date and compares what the card owes with what your bank and cash accounts hold ("You can pay this" or "Short by …"). Statement and due dates at a month end clamp to the last day.
  • Borrowing and repaying: borrowed money arrives in an account but is not income; paying it back leaves an account but is not spending (it was spent when borrowed).
  • Lending: the account balance drops, the budget does not. A repayment is tagged "Paid back", never income.
  • Self-transfers are paired only when they could really be one: compatible kinds, same bank reference (or none on either, within 2 minutes), never a salary without the same reference.

Money is integers

Every amount is an integer in paise (minor units). There is no floating-point money anywhere in storage or arithmetic. The largest accepted amount is ₹100 crore (1011 paise); parsing and calculation are guarded against overflow, and zero or negative input is rejected.
