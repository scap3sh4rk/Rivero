Most money apps tell you where it went.
Rivero tells you what is left.

Salary lands, rent and SIPs go out, cards and autopays are waiting. Rivero does the arithmetic you do in your head, every day, and keeps it honest against your real bank balance.

◎

One number, fully explained

Tap it and see every line: balance, money promised elsewhere, autopay due, card bills, days to payday. No black box.

⇄

A salary plan that moves money

Split your salary into spending, investments, savings, rent, family. Apply it and balances follow between your own accounts.

✦

Captures payments for you

Reads bank and UPI notifications on your phone (never SMS permissions), matches duplicates and asks only when unsure.

▭

Cards with their own billing cycle

Days left in each card's cycle, the due date, and whether your accounts can pay the bill. Autopay and loans tracked beside them.

☺

Owed, both ways

Split a dinner, lend or borrow money, record part-payments. One place for what people owe you, what you owe, and every bill to pay.

⚿

Locked and encrypted

AES-256 database, key in the phone's keystore, fingerprint or face lock, hide-amounts mode, encrypted backups only you can open.

Built around how salaries really work

Rivero models the way Indian households actually handle money, not a generic budget template.

Salary account is the source

Pay lands in one account. A monthly plan decides what goes to spending, investments, savings, rent, family and anything you add.

The bank balance is the truth

Safe to spend follows what your spending account really holds. Forgot to log a chai? The bank's balance message corrects the number anyway.

Self-transfers are not spending

Paying a card bill, moving money to savings, being repaid by a friend: none of it inflates your spending or your income.

Set up in the middle of the month

Already paid rent and invested this month? Say so once. Rivero never moves that money again and never drives an account negative.

Private by design, not by promise

On your phone

An encrypted SQLite database (AES-256). No server, no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking.

No SMS permission

Capture uses Android's notification access. Rivero never asks to read your messages, and it only keeps ones that look like money.

Backups you control

Encrypted on the phone with your passphrase before they go to your own Google Drive or a file. We cannot read them. Neither can Google.
