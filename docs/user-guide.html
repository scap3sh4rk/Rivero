User guide

Use Rivero, step by step

From first launch to a daily habit: set up your salary plan, capture payments automatically, and read your Safe to spend number with confidence.

Quick start

There is no setup wizard. Rivero opens straight to Home and a short guided tour walks you through every tab the first time, and opens the Plan, Goals and Autopay pages so you see where they are. You add things in place, in whatever order suits you.

  1. Install and open RiveroDownload the APK from the latest GitHub release and install it (allow "install unknown apps" for your browser once). The tour starts right away. Replay it any time from Settings → Take the tour.
  2. Add your accountsAccounts tab → Add account or card. Enter every bank account, wallet, cash and credit card you use, with today's balance. Cards also take a limit and their statement and due days.
  3. Set your salary planHome → Get started → Set your salary plan (or the Plan tile). Enter your salary and how it splits. The two steps tick themselves off on Home, and the daily number appears.
  4. Turn on capture (optional)Settings → Capture → Notification access, and choose your main UPI app. Rivero then records bank and UPI payments as they happen.
  5. Glance at HomeThe big number is what you can safely spend per day until payday.

Setting up in the middle of the month? The first time you apply a plan, Rivero asks "Has this month's money already moved?" Say yes if you already paid rent and invested: those categories are counted as paid and nothing is moved or deducted again.

Install on iPhone (experimental)

Rivero's main platform is Android. An experimental, unsigned iOS build is attached to each release as rivero-…-ios-unsigned.ipa. It is built and started in the iOS simulator by the project's CI, but it has not been tested on a real iPhone.

  1. Download the .ipaFrom the latest release.
  2. Sign it for your phoneApple only runs signed apps. With a free Apple ID, a sideloading tool such as AltStore or Sideloadly signs it for your own iPhone (it must be renewed every 7 days). With a paid Apple Developer account it can be signed for longer.
  3. Trust and openSettings → General → VPN & Device Management → trust your Apple ID, then open Rivero.

What is different on iPhone: iOS does not let apps read other apps' notifications, so there is no automatic payment capture. Log payments with the Log tab, scan bills, or import a CSV statement. The plan, Safe to spend, accounts, cards, owed money, autopay list, app lock (Face ID) and encrypted backups use the same code as Android. Google Drive backup on iPhone needs extra setup by the project; back up to a file for now.

The five tabs

Swipe left and right, or tap the bar at the bottom. Home is in the centre.

TabWhat it is for
OwedMoney in both directions: what people owe you, what you owe them, and every bill to pay (cards, loans, autopay).
AccountsBanks, cards, wallets, cash. Net worth, card usage, balances.
HomeSafe to spend per day, items to review, shortcuts.
LogA keypad to record a payment in two taps.
ActivityEverything that happened, with search, filters and a spending overview.

Home: your daily number

The big figure is Safe to spend per day. Below it: the total you can safely spend until payday and how many days that covers. The card itself is a battery: its background fills with how much of this cycle's spending money is left, green while there is plenty, amber as it runs down, brick red near empty. The fill is a light tint, so the text keeps one colour and reads clearly wherever the fill ends.

  • Tap the card to see "How this is calculated" with every line.
  • When the figures may be out of date a small label says so: Estimated last update 1 to 3 days ago, Stale older than 3 days. Fresh figures (under 24 hours) show no label.
  • Choose what is on Home (Settings → Appearance & Home → Home screen): Plan, Owed to you, Next autopay, Spent this cycle, Goals, Net worth, Card bills, You owe, in any order. Small cards sit two to a row and always have the same size; long names end in "…".
  • If the number shows "Short by", you have less spending money than you owe in bills before payday. Spending is not blocked; it is a warning.

To review

Every captured payment appears here first. Accept to count it, Ignore if it is not a payment, or tap it to pick a category and add a note first. (You can switch to automatic acceptance in Settings → Capture; even then, Rivero still asks when it had to guess which account was meant, or the message was unclear.)

Bank messages to check

If a money message could not be read, or a bank reports a failed autopay, a card appears with the message text. Choose Add it to record it by hand on the Log tab, or Not a payment to dismiss it.

Plan: where the salary goes

Open Plan from Home. The salary card shows your monthly salary and how it is split. Tap it to edit.

The salary plan screen

  • Salary arrives in and Spending money goes to pick your accounts. They can be the same account.
  • Set aside first lists categories (Investments, Savings, Rent, Family, anything you add). Leave one empty to skip it this month.
  • Tap a category's → to choose which of your accounts receives it, or "Paid outside Rivero" for rent, family and the like. If you have exactly one investment account, investments default to it.
  • The "Moves out of …" card lists every category with its amount and a total, so you can see the whole salary being spent.
  • Carry over unspent money adds last month's leftover spending money to this month.

What Apply does

Apply moves money only between accounts you track in Rivero: salary to spending, salary to a linked investment or savings account. Each move shows in Activity as "Transfer: SBI → ICICI".

Categories with no account of their own (rent, family) are plan-only. Nothing changes in your balances until the real payment is recorded: the bank's message arrives and matches the exact amount, or you tap the category on the Plan tab.

Setting up mid-month

The first time you apply in a cycle Rivero asks "Has this month's money already moved?"

Your answerWhat happens
Already movedNothing moves. Categories are marked done. Your entered balances stay as they are. Rivero remembers the answer for this cycle.
Move nowThe money moves between your accounts. Only offered when the salary account can cover it.

A plan never takes an account below zero. If the salary account holds less than the plan moves, Apply refuses and tells you why.

Marking a category paid

Tap a plan-only category on the Plan tab. Choose Already paid and pick the day you paid: the payment is added to Activity on that day and the category counts as paid, without changing the balance (it already shows the payment). Or choose Paying now: the amount comes out of the salary account today. Deleting that entry in Activity makes the category unpaid again.

Goals

At the bottom of the Plan tab (and on Home), Goals lets you save towards something ("Car, ₹5,00,000 by June"). Choose what counts towards it, in any mix: fixed deposits (with the interest earned so far), accounts and demat, what you pay into a plan category from now on, and cash, stocks, gold or anything else you value yourself (update the value when it changes). Each goal shows a progress ring, what each source adds, how much you still need each month, and whether you are on track or behind by ₹…. Tap a goal to edit it. Deleting is in the editor and asks first, with an Undo after. A goal never creates or moves money; it only reads it.

When salary arrives

If a salary credit is captured, Home offers to split it. A salary that lands a few days early (weekends, holidays) is treated as the next cycle's salary. Bonuses and refunds are only offered when the bank calls them salary.

Accounts

  • Tap the + at the bottom right to add an account or card: bank, wallet, cash, credit card, investment, or your own type. (Every page that adds something uses this same + in the same place.)
  • The eye icon at the top of the money pages hides or shows all amounts (Settings and Log have none). Each account also has its own eye, and it wins: with everything hidden, showing one account shows only that one (the others stay hidden).
  • Sections: Bank & cash, Investments & demat, Fixed deposits, Credit cards and Loans. Each section's name shows how many accounts it holds (no total, so hidden balances stay hidden). Tap a section's name (the small arrow) to fold it away; the arrange button at the top puts the sections in the order you like. Both are remembered.
  • Fixed deposits ask for the amount deposited, the interest rate, the day opened and the day it matures. Each shows its rate, when it matures (and in how many days) and what it will be worth then (interest added every quarter, in whole paise).
  • Net worth counts only the accounts you keep in it: switch "Count in net worth" off on an account to leave it out (it is still listed). Settings → Privacy & security → "Show net worth on Accounts" hides the card altogether.
  • Bank icons (Settings → Appearance & Home): add a picture once per bank, any bank, even a small one ("Another bank"), and every account, card and payment of that bank uses it. When adding or editing an account, Choose from your bank icons gives it any of those pictures. Rivero ships no bank logos.
  • Cards take a credit limit; the usage pill shows the percentage used, with ticks at 10% and 30%.
  • Billing cycle, per card: set the day the statement is generated, the day the bill is due and the account the bill is paid from. The statement day closes one bill and starts the next cycle that same day: with a statement on the 21st and payment due on the 5th, the bill generated on 21 Nov is due on 5 Dec, and what you spend from 21 Nov goes on the 21 Dec bill (due 5 Jan). Each card then shows days left in this billing cycle, Pay ₹<bill> by <date> (only that statement's bill, lowered by any payment since), what you spent since that goes on the next statement, overdue since <date> if a bill was not paid in time, and whether that account holds enough ("From HDFC Savings: enough" or "short by …"). Cards paid from the same account are judged together, against that account's balance ("with Amazon Pay, ₹18,000 in all"); cards with no paying account are judged together against your other money.
  • Tap an account to open it: balance, recent activity, autopay on that account. Update balance sets what it really holds, recorded as a visible "Balance update" entry.
  • Long-press and drag to reorder. Give each account a picture and a colour: 12 colours, each in four shades.
  • Tap Net worth for the breakdown.
  • Removing an account hides it; past transactions stay, and plan categories that pointed to it are un-linked.
  • Other currencies: a bank or cash account can be in USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD, CAD or AUD (cards stay in rupees). Its balance shows in its own currency. Set what one unit is worth in Settings → Exchange rates; totals, net worth, Safe to spend and spending then count it in rupees at that rate. Until a rate is set, the account is left out of totals and Accounts tells you so. Rates are typed in; Rivero never downloads them.

Log a payment

The Log tab is a calculator-style keypad. Type an amount (the calculator key handles things like 120 + 80 × 2), pick a category, optionally add a note in the small pill that appears under the amount once you have typed one, choose the account, and Save. The note pill never grows (its text scrolls as you type); while you type a note the number keypad folds away and comes back when you finish. Long-press 0 for a decimal point. Use the Income switch for money in.

  • If a spend is bigger than the account holds or than you have left to spend, Rivero asks Log this anyway?
  • Amounts above ₹100 crore are refused; zero and negative amounts are not accepted.
  • Scan a bill reads the total from a photo on your phone (on-device text recognition) and attaches the photo.

Activity and analytics

  • Search by merchant, category or note. Put #tags in a note ("Dinner #goa #office") and each tag becomes a chip above the list: tap one to see only those entries. The filter button sets the type (All, Expenses, Income, Transfers) and the date: today, yesterday, the last 7 days, this or last month, any of the last 12 months, a single day, or a from–to range. The chosen dates show as a chip you can clear.
  • Each day starts with a heading (Today, Yesterday, Sat, 14 Nov) and what you spent that day.
  • Tap an entry to edit the note and category, attach a bill photo, split the bill, confirm or ignore it, or delete it. Deleting undoes its effect on balances and budget.
  • The small overview at the top shows what you spent this cycle, or in the dates you picked; tap it for a daily chart and top categories. Analytics shows month by month and per account.

Owed: money in both directions

The Owed tab fits on one screen: two frames, Owed to you and You owe people, each with its total, how many people, the oldest entry and (for what you owe) card bills and autopay due, and a Net line below (for example: owed ₹10,000, owe ₹12,000, so Net: you owe ₹2,000). Tap a frame to open it. The + at the bottom right adds an entry (I lent money, or I borrowed money): who, how much, the date it happened (it can be logged later), the account or cash, and an optional photo of the bill or proof.

Owed to you

  • Everyone who owes you: split bills and loans. Tap a person to record a part-payment or full repayment; if it arrives in your bank, Rivero offers matching credits and tags the one you pick as "Paid back", never as income.
  • Split this bill (from any payment in Activity): their share stops counting as your spending immediately.
  • Write off gives up on what is left; for a loan, that remainder becomes spending.

You owe people

  • People you borrowed from, with who, how much, where it arrived and, optionally, the date you will pay it back. Borrowing is not income. Tap one to record paying back some or all of it, from an account or in cash (paying back is not spending). They forgave it closes a debt.
  • A debt dated before payday is held back from Safe to spend.
  • Bills & commitments is on the same page, in three groups: Credit cards (with what all cards owe in total beside the title), Loans and Autopay before payday. Each credit card shows only what matters: what is left to settle, the date to settle it by, and the account it is paid from with "enough" or "short by ₹…". Then each loan and every autopay due before payday. Tap a card to open it.
  • Settings → Money & Safe to spend → "Count money owed to me" optionally adds what is owed to you to Safe to spend before it arrives. Leave it off if friends repay into a different account than your spending account.

Autopay and cards

Autopay

Autopay lives on the Owed tab (the Autopay row under the Net line; Home's Next autopay card opens it too). It lists every recurring debit. Rivero adds them from bank messages ("mandate created", "will be debited on …") and by hand. Before payday it holds back what is still due, including every weekly debit left in the cycle, and the cap for variable-amount mandates.

  • When the real debit is recorded (captured or typed in), the autopay moves to its next date. Matching uses the payee name and the amount.
  • Kinds: each autopay is a SIP, subscription, loan EMI, insurance, bill, rent or other, with its own icon and colour, so a SIP never looks like Netflix. Rivero guesses from the name; tap an autopay → Edit to change the kind, payee, amount, how often, the account and the next date.
  • Overdue: an autopay whose date passed without its debit shows Overdue and stays kept aside in Safe to spend. Tap it to say what happened: It was paid (records the debit on its date; a SIP as an investment, not spending), It failed (keeps it aside until a retry date you pick), or Skip this one. If you say nothing, after 5 days it is assumed paid and moved on, unless the account it comes from holds less than the debit: then it most likely failed, so it stays overdue and kept aside until you decide.
  • Month-end dates are handled properly: a 31st rolls to the 28th or 29th in February.
  • Looks like autopay: when the same payee was paid a similar amount (within 10%) every month or every week, at least three times in the last six months, the Autopay page suggests it ("Netflix ₹649 monthly"). Add autopay registers it so Safe to spend keeps it aside; Not autopay stops suggesting it.

Credit cards

Card spending does not use up your spending budget when you swipe; it is held back as a bill to pay (Settings → Money & Safe to spend → "Keep money aside for card bills"). When you pay the bill from your bank, the bank's debit and the card's "payment received" are recognised as one transfer: the card's outstanding drops and your spending is not counted twice.

Keep card money in its own account? Many people move every card spend to a separate account (say, a Slice savings account) so the bill is always covered. Edit the card and set Bills paid from to that account. Safe to spend then counts only the part of the bill that account does not already hold, and the card's page shows whether the money set aside is enough for the bill or how much is short.

Capturing payments automatically

  1. Grant notification accessSettings → Capture → Open settings → enable Rivero. Rivero does not request SMS permission.
  2. Choose your main UPI appSettings → Capture → "My main UPI app" lists only the UPI apps installed on your phone. Its notifications are always read (even if it is an app Rivero does not know), and the amount is taken from the title when the body has none.
  3. Send a test notificationSettings → Capture → "Send a test notification". A "₹1 spent" notification appears: tap it to try the quick-add popup. The payment also waits under To review on Home.
  4. Let it workBank SMS (via your messaging app), bank apps and UPI apps are listened to. Only notifications that mention money are kept; chats and OTPs are not.

Captured messages wait in an encrypted queue on the phone until Rivero opens, then are processed in order. Old raw message text is deleted after 90 days.

Add a payment from its notification

When Rivero captures a payment and you are not in the app, your phone shows a notification such as ₹450 spent. Tap it (or Add) and a small popup opens over whatever you are doing: pick a category, type a note if you like (the popup moves up above the keyboard), and press Add or Ignore. Rivero does not even need to open. On a locked phone the notification shows only "Payment to add", and your phone asks you to unlock before the popup opens. Open Rivero instead opens the app on that payment. One-time passwords, reminders and statement notices never notify. On Android 13 and newer, allow Rivero to show notifications when asked.

Accept or ignore each payment

By default every captured payment waits on Home under To review for your Accept or Ignore. Nothing counts, or counts twice, until you say so, and duplicates are easy to spot. If you prefer, turn on Settings → Capture → Accept payments automatically; then only confident, certain captures go straight through.

Rules

Settings → Rules sorts captured payments for you: when the payee or message contains some text (and, optionally, the amount is in a range), put it in a category, or ignore it. The first matching rule wins. A payment sorted by a rule still waits for your Accept unless automatic accept is on. After you file the same payee in the same category twice, Rivero offers to Make a rule.

When a payment does not show up

Open Settings → Capture → Recent captures. It lists every notification Rivero received and what it did (recorded, ignored and why, or could not read), plus counters from the phone: how many notifications from your payment and bank apps had money in them, how many did not, and how many had hidden text. If text is hidden, set your phone's notifications to show content for your messages and UPI apps. Home also shows a card while notification access is off.

A message Rivero could not read appears under Bank messages to check on Home. Report this format helps fix it for everyone: it shows the message with every digit, UPI ID and name hidden, lets you remove anything else personal, and then opens a filled-in GitHub report in your browser. Nothing is sent unless you submit it there.

Reminders

Settings → Reminders (Android) sends phone reminders: a credit card bill every day from 5 days before it is due until the due date, and every day while it is overdue (with whether your accounts cover it), an autopay the day before (warning if the account is short), money you owe the day before you said you would pay it back, and a nudge when someone has owed you for 30 days. Each kind can be switched off. Reminders are planned on the phone and survive a restart.

Battery: some phones stop background apps. Settings → Capture shows Battery: keep capture running; set Rivero to Unrestricted so capture and reminders keep working. Before opening notification access, Rivero explains exactly what it reads and that nothing leaves the phone.

How Rivero decides

  • One-time passwords, pre-debit reminders, mandate notices and card statements are never recorded as payments.
  • Two messages for one payment (a UPI app and the bank) become one entry. Different UPI references are always different payments.
  • A payment you already typed in, up to 6 hours earlier, is matched with the bank's message instead of being counted twice.
  • Promotional messages ("Get ₹500 cashback…") are ignored.
  • The bank's "available balance" corrects the account's balance when a captured payment is confirmed.

Backup, restore, lock

  • Backup & restore: save to your own Google Drive or to a file. You choose a passphrase (8+ characters); the backup is encrypted on the phone before it leaves. Bill photos and account pictures are included; raw bank message text is not. Keep your passphrase safe: it cannot be recovered.
  • Automatic backup: Backup & restore → "Automatic backup to Google Drive": daily or weekly. It uses the passphrase you set once (kept in the phone's secure storage) and runs when you open Rivero and a backup is due. The previous copy is kept too, so one bad backup never replaces the only good one. If no backup was made for two weeks, Home reminds you.
  • Restore replaces everything on the phone, and refuses a backup made by a newer version of the app.
  • Export data (Settings → Backup & data): everything as an Excel workbook (one sheet each for transactions, accounts, plan, owed and autopay) or as CSV files in a .zip. Amounts are exact; nothing is uploaded, you choose where to save it.
  • App lock: fingerprint, face or phone PIN to open. While on, screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked.
  • Hide amounts shows dots instead of numbers.
  • Delete all data wipes the database, photos and the capture queue, then starts setup again.

Settings you may want

Settings (the profile button at the top right) is a short list of groups, each opening its own page: Appearance & Home (theme, fonts, Home cards, bank icons, open on quick log), Money & Safe to spend (pay day, card bills, money owed, keep untouched, exchange rates), Categories & rules (categories, capture rules, account types), Capture and Reminders (Android), Privacy & security (hide amounts, app lock, net worth card) and Backup & data (backup, export, import, delete all). Take the tour sits on the first page.

Forms: every input has a faint outline and space around it, and every list of choices opens as a dropdown right under its field (as wide as the field, scrolling when long, never past the screen edges).

FAQ

Why is my Safe to spend lower than my plan says?

Your bank balance is the truth. If the spending account holds less than the plan expected (for example you set up mid-month), that is what you can spend. Tap the card to see the lines.

I paid rent but my salary account did not change.

Rent has no account in Rivero, so it is plan-only. Tap the category on the Plan tab and choose Paying now, or let the bank's message match it.

A payment I made was not captured.

Check notification access is on and run the test notification. Look for a "bank messages to check" card on Home. You can always add the payment on the Log tab.

The balance looks wrong.

Open the account and use Update balance. The difference is recorded as a "Balance update" so history stays explainable.

I skipped the tour. Where is it?

Settings → Take the tour. The Home "Get started" card also reminds you of the two steps the daily number needs: add your accounts and set your salary plan.

Does it work on iPhone?

There is an experimental unsigned iOS build (see "Install on iPhone"). It builds and starts in the iOS simulator, but it has not been tested on a real iPhone, and iOS cannot read notifications, so payments are logged by hand, scanned from bills or imported from a statement.

Is my data safe if I lose my phone?

It is encrypted and can be behind your fingerprint. Make regular encrypted backups; restoring on a new phone brings everything back, including photos.
